This policy explains what information **RestroOne** ("we", "us") collects, why, and what we do with it when you visit our website or use our restaurant software (the "Service").

## 1. Information we collect

- **Account details:** your name, email address, mobile number, password (stored only as a one-way hash), and your business's name, address, city, state, PIN code and logo.
- **Location:** if you press "Use my location" while registering, your browser shares your coordinates with us, and the name of the place is looked up from them. We store the coordinates with your business.
- **Business data you enter:** menu items, prices, orders, bills, tables, stock, suppliers, staff and your customers' details (name, phone, address). This is your data; we hold it for you.
- **Payments:** the plan you chose, invoices, and payment references. Card details are handled by the payment provider and are not stored by us.
- **Technical data:** device and browser type, IP address, pages used, sign-in history and error logs, to keep the Service secure and working.
- **Messages:** what you send us through Support or the Contact support form.

## 2. Why we use it

- to create and run your account, and to confirm your email address with a one-time code;
- to provide the Service, invoice you and take payments;
- to send you service emails: the code, your welcome email, trial and renewal notices, password resets;
- to keep the Service safe, prevent misuse, and fix faults;
- to answer your questions and improve the product.

We do not sell your information.

## 3. Who we share it with

Only the providers that help us run the Service, and only what they need: web hosting, email delivery, payment processing, and place-name lookup for the location button. We may also disclose information if the law or a court requires it. Staff you add to your account can see the data their role allows.

## 4. Cookies

We use cookies that are needed to keep you signed in and to protect forms against forgery, and a few to remember your preferences (for example light or dark theme). We do not use them for advertising.

## 5. How long we keep it

While your account is active, and for up to 30 days after it ends so that you can export your data. After that we delete it, except records we must keep by law (such as invoices and payment records).

## 6. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS, passwords are hashed, access is limited by role, and every business's data is kept apart from every other's. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You can see and correct your details in your profile, export your business data, and ask us to delete your account. To exercise any right, contact us through Support.

## 8. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not meant for people under 18.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date shows when. If a change matters, we will tell you.

## 10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to us through the Support section of your account or the contact details on our website.
