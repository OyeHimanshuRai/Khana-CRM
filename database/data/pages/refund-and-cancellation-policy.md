This policy explains how cancellation and refunds work for **RestroOne** plans.

## 1. Free trial

A free trial costs nothing and needs no card. When it ends, the account is locked until you pay for a plan. We do not charge you automatically at the end of a trial.

## 2. Cancelling

You may stop using RestroOne at any time. Plans are paid in advance and do not renew by themselves, so to cancel simply do not renew. Your access continues until the end of the period you have paid for.

## 3. Refunds

- **First payment:** if you are not satisfied, tell us **within 7 days** of your first paid plan starting and we will refund that payment in full, if the plan has not been used for live billing in that time.
- **After that:** payments for a period already begun are not refundable, because the Service has been made available to you for that period.
- **Charged by mistake or twice:** if you were charged in error, we will correct it or refund the extra amount.
- **Upgrades:** when you move to a higher plan during a period, the unused part of your current plan is credited toward the new one.

## 4. How a refund is paid

An approved refund is returned to the payment method that was used, or by bank transfer for payments recorded manually, within 7 to 10 working days. Any GST on the refunded amount is reversed with a credit note.

## 5. Termination by us

If we end your account because of a breach of the [Terms and Conditions](/p/terms-and-conditions), the amount paid for the rest of the period is not refunded. If we end it for any other reason, we will refund the unused part of the period.

## 6. How to ask

Write to us through the Support section of your account, or the contact details on our website, with your business name and the invoice number.
